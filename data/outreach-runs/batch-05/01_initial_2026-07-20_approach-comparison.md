# batch-05 - initial (touch 1) - approach comparison: current engine vs COLD_OPENER_TEARDOWN.md

Purpose: regenerate every touch-1 asset for the 10 batch-05 banking leads under the revised approach
in `/COLD_OPENER_TEARDOWN.md`, side by side with what actually shipped on 2026-07-20, so we can judge
the new approach message by message before adopting it.

Status note: batch-05 touch 1 already SENT on 2026-07-20 (see `01_initial_2026-07-20.md`). This file
is an evaluation, not a re-send. If the new approach is approved, apply it from batch-06 onward and to
batch-05 touch 2.

## What changed, and the teardown item behind each change

- **(A) Trigger-first.** The current copy already leads with the researched hook, so this stays. Where
  possible the new copy tightens it to the very first clause.
- **(B) Assertive about us, tentative about them.** New copy adds one hedged hypothesis about the
  prospect's situation ("if I've read it right", "if it's like other banks I see"). We stay flat and
  factual about our own capability. This is the single biggest change.
- **(C) Question as the close.** The current CTA "Would a short call be worth your time?" is a soft
  meeting ask. New copy closes on one open diagnostic question instead.
- **(E) Verified-proof-swap gate.** Unchanged here. The current copy already uses only verified,
  geography-annotated references (Meezan, İşbank/Akbank, Fibabanka 4.45M), no GCC logo, GCP Dammam for
  KSA. New copy keeps them, subordinated under the prospect's specifics.
- **(I) Founder-voice honesty.** The sender is Salil (Sr. Director, Middle East), so the new copy uses
  a lighter, comparing-notes register, not the "I'm growing a CDP" founder frame from the sample set.

Deliberately NOT applied, and why:
- **(D) Honest incumbent wedge.** Requires a martech/CDP stack we can actually observe. None of these
  10 banks has disclosed one (their signals are core-banking and digital-front-end: Backbase, Msa3ed,
  NBO engine, NBF Direct). Naming any of those as a marketing incumbent would be dishonest, so the
  wedge is replaced by the diagnostic question. When a real martech incumbent is visible (a job post,
  their site, enrichment), the wedge applies; not here.
- **(G) Channel.** Delivery is unchanged (LinkedIn manual + email 1:1). Community/event warm entry is
  a sourcing change, not a copy change.

Content rules held throughout: no em/en dashes, no rule-of-three as a rhetorical tell, no filler,
"Hi <First>," greeting, "Best, Salil" sign-off, plain text.

## Read on the comparison

- The new approach barely touches the trigger or the proof, which the current engine already does well
  for banking. The change is concentrated in two places: one hedged line about the prospect's
  situation, and a diagnostic question in place of the meeting ask.
- Strongest expected lift: contact 8 (Ziad, CRM/data owner) and the persona-owning questions (4, 6,
  10), where the closing question maps to something the buyer literally runs.
- Weakest fit: contact 1 (Hassan, comms/PR seat). Neither version fixes a soft persona match; the
  standing flag to find a retail/digital contact at SNB still holds.
- The incumbent wedge, the single most common winner in the sample set, is not usable on this batch
  because no martech incumbent is observable. It will matter most on prospects where enrichment or a
  job posting reveals the stack (Insider, Netcore, WebEngage, Braze, Salesforce Marketing Cloud).
